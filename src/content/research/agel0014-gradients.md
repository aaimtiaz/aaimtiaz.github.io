For my M.Sc. thesis I investigated the stellar populations of AGEL0014, a strongly lensed quiescent galaxy at z ≈ 1.4. I reduced and analysed Gemini/GSAOI, HST and Keck/MOSFIRE data, troubleshooting the DRAGONS pipeline by modifying local scripts to handle low source counts.

I then performed multiband lens modelling and used Lenstronomy's ray-shooting method to map image-plane segments to the source plane for spatially resolved spectral analysis.

Finally, I performed SED fitting with `alf` and Prospector to measure metallicity and age gradients, providing insight into galaxy formation and quenching. Intensive computations were performed on the NARIT HPC cluster.

A first-author paper is in preparation.
