In this CANUCS collaboration project, we investigate the physical properties of compact stellar systems ("sparkles") within strongly lensed galaxies. I began this work during the ICTP PWF Physics for Bangladesh Online Internship and have continued it as a Graduate Research Assistant at CASSA.

My primary contribution is modelling the spectral energy distributions of the sparkles across 43 galaxies in five lensing clusters. Using Dense Basis, I estimate both Simple Stellar Population and complex stellar-population properties.

This work contributes to a collaboration paper now in preparation under the supervision of Dr. Lamiya Mowla.
